## Project

A web game for the Bio-Streams (EU) science competition. The topic is **childhood obesity**. The player cares for an avatar who is a school student and tries to raise its health (1 to 5) through **sport, sleep and food** (food is secondary only in the order of development).
The author is a 9th-grade student competing in the national phase. Goal: win and present in Athens. The game must be scientifically sound, and everything in this repository is **public and free for anyone to use**.

## Technical decisions (fixed)

- **Godot 4.6.3**, **GDScript**, **Compatibility renderer** (`gl_compatibility`). Do not use Forward+ or Mobile (the dev Mac is Intel with weak graphics, and Compatibility is the only one for web). Do not upgrade Godot.
- **Platform: web page for all devices** (computer, Android, iOS in the browser). Native apps are optional and only if there is time. **Test first on the Mac.**
- Portrait base resolution **720 x 1280**. On wide screens the game stays in a **centred column**. Use Control nodes with anchors and containers; stretch mode `canvas_items`, aspect `keep`.
- **No server, no accounts, no notifications, no daily limit.** The first version saves locally on the device. Keep **time and saving in separate scripts** so a server can replace them later without changing the rest.
- Web build is exported to the `docs/` folder and published with **GitHub Pages**. Keep documentation in `documentation/`, never in `docs/`.
- Data-driven: foods, activities and all tunable numbers live in `data/` (JSON), not in code.
- Texts go through Godot translations (`translations/`, CSV) with **pt** and **en**. Language is chosen in settings. Wording is scientifically correct and not simplified for small children.
- Placeholder art only for now: simple shapes (the avatar is an egg shape). Plasticine art is optional, later.

## Game rules (confirmed)

**Measures**
- Health: 1 to 5, **calculated from the measures**, not fixed. Health = 1 + 4 × the **power mean** of Physical, Happiness and Sleep with **p = 0.5** and equal weights (v1 Step 11; built in Milestone 10 Task 8, `scripts/health/health_model.gd`). The target health follows the measures every step; the displayed health (the bar) moves `health.alpha_bar` (0.37) of the way to it once per avatar day, when a sleep ends (decision 1). The formula gives the **target health**. The **displayed health** (the health bar) starts at the target and then moves part of the way toward it once per avatar day, when a sleep ends (`health.alpha_bar`, 0.37, placeholder). During the day health does not change. The **health level** is health rounded to a whole number (1 to 5).
- Physical: 0 to 1 (0.44 on Normal in v1). v1 Step 8: Physical = (0.5·√w + 0.3·√fitness + 0.2·√strength)² × (0.4 + 0.6·Q) × (1 − 0.5·U) × (1 − 0.3·O), with w the weight score from the WHO BMI-for-age z, Q diet quality, U energy deficit penalty, O overreaching. Built in Milestone 10 Task 8; worked out every step. Its values are kept separately in `GameState`: **fitness** and **muscle** (0 to 1, slow body states that change once per avatar day, see "Health calculation (working plan)"), the **weight z-score**, and **calories spent** (total kcal spent by exercise, never reset; a record only). Fitness takes the place of the planned "endurance".
- Mental is renamed **Happiness** in v1: 0 to 1, 0.50 on Normal. It moves toward a baseline (from Physical, Sleep, diet quality, fun, deficit, screen excess, overreaching) with a 12-avatar-hour time constant and jumps with events (meals, exercise, football, screens, hunger, waking); k does not apply (v1 Step 10). It replaces the Milestone 4 mood and habit-change cost model.
- Sleep: 0 to 1, 0.50 on Normal. v1 Step 9: Sleep = (1 − min(1, debt ÷ 8)) × (1 − min(0.3, 0.1 × oversleep)); the sleep debt factor d slows training gains, raises appetite and lowers exercise capacity. Sleep never changes the metabolic rate.
- The starting measures and muscle come from the chosen difficulty (`health.difficulties` in `data/config.json`). Before the player chooses, the Normal values are used.
- XP: unlimited (v1 Step 12, built in Milestone 10 Task 8, `scripts/health/progress.gd`; values in the config group `progress`, placeholders). Every change of the displayed health gives or takes `per_health_point` (100) XP per point. Every avatar hour while the displayed health is at least `high_health_min` (4.5) gives `gain_per_avatar_hour` (10) x (1 + hours at that level without a break / `bonus_hours` (48)), at most x `max_multiplier` (3). There is no hourly XP loss at low health. XP never goes below 0. The level is XP ÷ `xp.per_level` (rounded down), so losing XP can lower the level; a new level shows "Level up!" in the speech bubble for a few seconds.
- Sport and sleep drive progression. Food is built after them.
- **Player choices:** age group (child or teenager), sex (male or female) and difficulty. The age sets the recommended sleep range (child 9 to 12 h, teenager 8 to 10 h; placeholder) and the sex sets the WHO BMI-for-age tables. The player chooses them, with the avatar's name, on the **avatar creation screen**, which opens when there is no save; they are saved in `GameState`. Nothing is saved before Start is pressed.
- **Old saves do not load.** The save has a version number (`SaveGame.SAVE_VERSION`); a save of another version is ignored, the creation screen opens and the player starts a new avatar (the old save is replaced when the new avatar is saved). Version 2 = Milestone 10 (v1 health system): saves from Milestone 4 or earlier (version 1) do not load (decision 6, user, 25 Oct 2026). A save without the creation choices is ignored too. Every GameState value is saved, except the two per-step values `energy_spent_step` and `beyond_capacity_minutes_step`, which are worked out again every step; the Clock, the running activity, the action log and Simulation's pending minutes are saved too (`tests/save_test.gd`).
- **Difficulty and starting values (v1, placeholders; Physical, Happiness, Sleep → health):** Easy (0.71, 0.75, 0.75) → 3.94; Normal (0.44, 0.50, 0.50) → 2.91; Hard (0.14, 0.30, 0.30) → 1.95, obesity, habits cheap to change; Very hard (0.03, 0.15, 0.10) → 1.35, severe obesity, very bad habits (starting happiness 0.15, not the concept's 0.05: open decision 1). Each difficulty also sets z, fitness, strength, diet quality Q, craving C and sleep debt (table in the v1 document). Starting health comes from these measures.
- **Planned health calculation:** the full working plan is in the section "Health calculation (working plan)" below. The structure is decided, **all numbers are placeholders**. A first version was built in Milestone 3; Milestone 4 (`MILESTONE_4.md`) audits it and brings it in line with the team's values.
- **Exercise limits (v1 Step 6).** Daily capacity = (60 + 90 × fitness) × (1 − 0.3 × d) minutes; minutes beyond it give no benefit, cost a little happiness and build an overreaching load O. Strength started before 60% recovery (24 h time constant) counts as too soon. "Tired" (tiredness) comes from sleep debt and hours awake, not from exercise.

**Time**
- Day = **20 real minutes** = 14 avatar hours (1 real minute = 42 avatar minutes; v1; `time.day_avatar_minutes_per_real_minute` in config, built in Milestone 10 Task 2).
- Night = 2 real hours = 10 avatar hours of sleep (1 real minute = 5 avatar minutes). Sleeping less is proportional (8 h of sleep = 96 real minutes).
- **Sleep-to-day conversion (confirmed).** The baseline is 10 hours of sleep and a 14-hour day (24 hours in total). Hours slept below 10 are **added to the next day** and hours slept above 10 are **taken from it**: `day_hours = 14 + (10 - sleep_hours)`. Example: 5 hours of sleep give a 19-hour day, and 12 hours of sleep give a 12-hour day. The day's real length uses the same scale: `day_real_minutes = day_hours * 60 / 42` (19 h is about 27.1 real minutes, 14 h is 20 real minutes). The 10 h only sets the scale; recommended sleep comes from the age (child target 10.5 h, teenager 9 h). The first day is 14 hours.
- Implement this as one small, well-tested conversion function in `Clock` (avatar hours to real seconds, and back), with the numbers read from `Config`. Put the maximum sleep on the slider in config (placeholder, for example 12 hours).
- A **24-hour clock** in the interface (in the top-right column, under the XP level box, above the pause button) shows the avatar's time of day. Placeholder: the first day starts at 07:00. With this conversion bedtime stays at the same clock time and the wake-up time moves with the hours slept.
- Game time only advances while the game is open. Closing the tab pauses time.
- A pause button stops the day. The night starts when the day ends, but sleep is **not automatic**: the avatar gets tired, shows a recommendation to sleep, and the player decides. When sleep ends, the game pauses and a **wake button** starts a new day.
- Sleep is optional and variable (a slider chooses the hours). Not sleeping lowers health significantly.
- **When the avatar can sleep (v1 Step 9).** Only when its sleep debt is at least 1 h or it has been awake at least 6 avatar hours; otherwise it says it is not sleepy. It wakes after the chosen hours, never later than the maximum plus min(debt, 2 h). Within that rule the sleep button works at any moment of the day, not only at bedtime. Built in Milestone 10 Task 2 (`Clock.is_sleepy()`, `Clock.max_sleep_hours()`; until Task 6 the debt is the Milestone 4 `sleep_debt`); pressing Sleep when not sleepy shows "I'm not sleepy." and the slider stops at the longest sleep allowed. A nap is simply a short sleep and follows the same rule: when any sleep ends, a new day starts whose length is `14 + (10 - hours slept)`, so one sleep plus the awake period after it always adds up to 24 hours.
- **Ignoring the sleep recommendation (confirmed).** If the awake period runs out and the player has not slept, it counts as **0 hours slept**: a new 24-hour day starts at once, the avatar stays tired and the sleep measure keeps falling. The recommendation stays visible, and the player can still sleep at any time.

**Food and mood** (build later)
- The player chooses what the avatar eats when hungry (4 to 5 meals; menus decided later).
- Junk food always lowers physical health but supports mental health. Disliked food makes the avatar sad.
- A gradual junk-to-healthy transition keeps mental health stable and lets physical health recover. Without a transition, mental health falls and the avatar can enter a severe mental-illness state that lowers overall health.

**Failure**
- **Team decision (22 Oct 2026): there is no death, only failure.** v1 Step 12 (to tune): failure when health is below 1.3 for 24 avatar hours, happiness below 0.10 for 12 avatar hours, or an irreversible disease (disease framework only; which diseases is not decided). The effect is not defined yet; show a placeholder message. Do not build any death system. Built in Milestone 10 Task 8 (failure timers in `GameState`; the disease part is only a risk index and hidden damage in the debug panel, decision 9).

## Health calculation (working plan)

Source: "Architecture Decisions: Health System" (see Documents) and the team's "Plano de Investigação e Testes: Sistema de Saúde". The structure is decided; numbers not yet researched use an **average placeholder** in `data/config.json`, marked as a placeholder, so the team can change them at any time. A first version was built in **Milestone 3** (see "Built so far" below). **Milestone 4** (`MILESTONE_4.md` has the config keys and values) audits that code and brings it in line with this section; until a Milestone 4 task changes a part, the Milestone 3 behaviour described below is what the code does.

**Idea.** Health (1 to 5) is not set by hand. It comes from three measures (Physical, Mental, Sleep, each 0 to 1), which come from habits compared with scientific recommendations.

**Player choices and recommendations**
- Age group: child (reference age 10) or teenager (reference age 14). Sex: male or female (changes the WHO BMI-for-age tables and, later, energy use).
- Recommended sleep: child (6 to 12 years) 9 to 12 h per 24 h; teenager (13 to 18 years) 8 to 10 h. Recommended activity for both: at least 60 min/day of moderate-to-vigorous activity.
- Difficulty and starting measures: see "Difficulty and starting values" above. Starting health is **calculated** from those measures with the formula below, never set by hand. The "Hard" difficulty has low cost of changing habits; "Very hard" has high cost (`habit_change_cost`: 1 normal, 0.5 Hard, 2 Very hard; placeholders).
- **Starting body states** (in `health.difficulties`, placeholders) are chosen so that on day 1 they give the starting measures, and health does not jump after the first sleep: (fitness + muscle + weight score) ÷ 3 = physical, with fitness = muscle; mood = mental; sleep debt = 0. The weight score is 1 for z between −1 and +1 and falls to 0 at ±3 along a curve (section `physical_measure`): t² where t goes in a straight line from 1 at ±1 to 0 at ±3, so obesity (z = +2) already gives 0.25. The curve power is a placeholder; with a straight line the starting fitness of Very hard would be higher than Hard. The usual level of each habit is kept as a habit score (0 to 1): sleep = starting sleep, activity = fitness, strength = muscle, screens = starting mental.

> **Superseded design.** The plan in this section (k = 5, Mental/mood with the habit-change cost, Physical = average of three, p 0.2, once-per-day update) described the Milestone 4 code, which Milestone 10 Tasks 1 to 8 have **replaced by v1** (`documentation/health_calculation_architecture_v1.md`); the paragraphs marked "built in Milestone 10" below describe the code now. The rest of this section is an old record until Milestone 10 Task 11 rewrites it.

**Body speed.** v1 (team, 25 Oct 2026): **k = 10** for everything metabolic, sleep debt included (weight, body age, fitness level, strength level, diet quality, craving, sleep-debt recovery, disease damage). k does **not** apply to the clock, hours slept, minutes per day, sessions per week, recovery between sessions, meal timing, habituation or happiness. (Code now: k = 5, run as k real-day updates per avatar day, not on sleep debt.)

**Six steps, run once per avatar day at wake-up** (steps 1 and 6 also run during the day):
1. **Action log:** every activity is logged with the avatar's time and its duration.
2. **Habit scores (0 to 1):** compare the last day (and the last 7 days for weekly rules) with the recommendation. 0 when far too little, 1 inside the healthy range, lower again when too much. Doing more gives no extra points.
   - Sleep s(S): 1 inside the range for the age; falls below it (0 at 4 h or less) and, slowly, above it.
   - Activity a(A) = minutes of moderate-to-vigorous activity ÷ 60, at most 1.
   - Strength w = strength days in the last 7 ÷ 3, at most 1 (vigorous activity uses the same rule).
   - Screens c(T) = 1 up to 2 h of recreational screens per day; falls to 0 (placeholder: 6 h).
3. **Slow body states** (change only part of the way each day, using k):
   - Sleep debt: new = old × r + (recommended sleep − hours slept), never below 0. r sets how fast debt is forgiven (undecided).
   - Fitness and muscle: new = fitness + k_up × a × (1 − fitness) − k_down × (1 − a) × fitness.
   - Weight: WHO BMI-for-age z-score, z = ((BMI ÷ M)^L − 1) ÷ (L × S), with L, M, S from the WHO tables for age and sex. Overweight above +1, obesity above +2.
   - Mood: moves toward a level set by sleep, activity, enjoyment, food and too much screen time.
   - Usual level of each habit: moves slowly toward what the avatar does; used for the cost of changing habits.
4. **Measures (0 to 1):**
   - Physical = mix of fitness, muscle and a weight score (1 when z is between −1 and +1, falling toward 0 near ±3). The mix is undecided.
   - Sleep = the day's sleep score, lowered by sleep debt.
   - Mental = mood ÷ 100 (mood on a 0 to 100 scale), lowered by the cost of changing habits quickly (doing much more than the usual level lowers mental health; changing step by step costs little; same rule as the gradual food transition).
   - **Habit change cost (team model, 22 Oct 2026).** For each habit, with A = today's intensity (0 to 1), H = usual level (0 to 1), R = rootedness (0 to 1, independent of H): D = max(0, |A − H| − T); P = min(P_max, α_habit × factor × R^γ × D^γ); mood = max(0, mood − P); R grows R += λ(1 − R) only while the habit is stable. Values after the gradual-vs-sudden solution (user decision, 24 Oct 2026; Google Doc "Habit-change cost: gradual vs sudden change"): T 0.10, α_habit 200, γ 2, P_max 100 points per habit per day with no cap on the day's total, factor 0 for sleep and 1 for the other habits, λ 0.04 (before: α 10, γ 1.5, P_max 10 on the total). H reaches 95% of a new habit in about 66 real days (Lally 2010, k applied). Keep α_habit separate from the health-bar α.
5. **Target health** = 1 + 4 × C, where C combines the three measures (below).
6. **Displayed health** moves part of the way toward the target each day: new health = health + α × (target − health). α is undecided (example 0.3 per day). The bar changes slowly; messages and animations give immediate feedback.

**Combining the measures: weighted power mean**

`C = (w_P · P^p + w_M · M^p + w_S · S^p)^(1/p)`, with `w_P + w_M + w_S = 1`.

- p = 1 is the ordinary average (a strong measure fully makes up for a weak one). p close to 0 is the geometric mean (the weakest measure pulls everything down).
- Placeholder: p = 0.5 and equal weights (1/3 each).
- Example (physical 0.9, mental 0.6, sleep 0.1): ordinary average gives health 3.1, p = 0.5 gives 2.9, geometric mean gives 2.5.
- p and the weights are **calibrated** with test scenarios taken from the recommendations, not invented. Example scenario: "sleeping 5 hours every day can never give health above 3, even with lots of exercise". Write these scenarios as tests when the system is built.
- Sleep is not counted twice: it already lowers mental and physical health through steps 3 and 4, so the weights stay almost equal.
- **Config (Milestone 4 Task 1).** Every health value is in one `health` block in `data/config.json`, with the key names of `MILESTONE_4.md`. Each value is written as `{"value": ..., "status": "placeholder" | "team decision" | "from source", "owner": "Pessoa 1 to 4" or "team"}`; `Config.get_value()` returns its value and `Config.is_placeholder()` is true for status "placeholder". Sub-blocks: `habit_cost`, `sleep_range`, `sleep_recommended`, `weight_score`, `physical_mix`, `combine`, `log` (the log types and 24 h / 7 days), `difficulties` (starting values and `start_R` per difficulty) and `milestone3_rules` (old rules still in use, deleted in Milestone 4 Task 5). The age group key is `teenager` (MILESTONE_4.md writes `teen`), because the game saves it that way. Cardio and muscle strength have `kcal_per_hour` (300 and 200, a record only) and `activity_intensity` ("moderate_vigorous") in `data/activities.json`. **In debug builds, F6 reloads `config.json` while the game runs**; the next calculation uses the new values. No health number is left in a script (`tests/config_test.gd` checks this).
- **Built so far (Milestone 3 code, now reading the `health` block; Milestone 4 Tasks 2 to 8 still change it: k run as k real-day updates, the team habit-cost model with rootedness R, mood 0 to 100, enjoyment, the scoring day, failure, CSV log):** the health formula, the starting values per difficulty, the action log and habit scores (`Habits`), the slow body states (`GameState`), the measures and the daily health step, and a debug panel (`scenes/ui/debug_panel.tscn`, F7) that shows the habit scores, body states, usual habit levels, measures, target and displayed health. The debug panel only exists in debug builds (the editor); it removes itself from the published release build.
- **How the action log and scoring days work** (`scripts/autoload/habits.gd`, Milestone 4 Task 2): every activity that ends, and every sleep, is logged with its type (the activity option id: cardio, strength, entertainment, sleep), its scoring day, the avatar time it started (avatar hours since the game began, clock day number and time of day), the avatar hours actually done (Stop counts only the time done), its `activity_intensity` and its calories (`kcal_per_hour` × hours). A **scoring day** follows `health.score_window` = `wake_to_wake`: it runs from one wake-up to the next, so it closes when any sleep ends (a night, a nap, or the 0-hour rule, which counts as 0 h of sleep). When it closes, its totals are worked out (`Habits.day_totals()`): sleep hours, moderate-to-vigorous minutes (entries whose intensity is in `log.activity_intensities`), strength days in the last 7 scoring days (this one included; two sessions on one day count once), entertainment hours, recreational screen hours (`log.screen_types`; all entertainment for now) and calories. The totals give five scores (Milestone 4 Task 3, each 0 to 1): sleep s (1 inside the age range; linear from 0 at `sleep_zero_at` = 4 h to 1 at the lower limit; −`sleep_over_penalty_per_h` = 0.1 per hour above the upper limit), activity a = minutes ÷ `activity_target_min` (60), strength w = strength days ÷ `strength_days_target` (3), screens c (1 up to `screens_full_until_h` = 2 h, linear to 0 at `screens_zero_at_h` = 6 h) and enjoyment e = entertainment hours ÷ `enjoyment_full_at_h` (1), each at most 1. Enjoyment is only stored for now; it joins the mood target in Task 5. The scores are kept in `Habits.scores` and passed to `GameState.update_body_states()`; then the next scoring day starts. The log keeps the last 7 scoring days (`log.log_days`) and the totals of the last 7 closed days (`Habits.days`); all of it is saved. Entries from a Milestone 3 save count in the current scoring day.
- **How the slow body states work** (`GameState.update_body_states()`, Milestone 4 Task 4, numbers in the `health` block): once per scoring day, right after the habit scores, at the end of any sleep. The states that use k run the **real-day update k times** (k = 5, rounded to a whole number; `GameState.real_days_per_avatar_day()`), so one avatar day counts as 5 real days for the body and never overshoots. k is never applied to the clock, the sleep, the scores or mood, and to sleep debt only if `k_applies_to_sleep_debt` is true (it is false).
  - Fitness (from the activity score a) and muscle (from the strength score w), per real day: x += k_up·score·(1 − x) − k_down·(1 − score)·x, with k_up and k_down 0.0056; run k times.
  - Sleep debt (hours), once per avatar day: new = old × r + (recommended sleep for the age group − hours slept), never below 0; r = 0.8; recommended = the **lower limit** of the range (child 9 h, teenager 8 h; Task 6.5, team decision), so sleep inside the recommended range adds no debt. With enough sleep it halves in about 3 avatar days.
  - **Mood (0 to 100, `mood_max`) and the habit-change cost** (Milestone 4 Task 5, team model, `health.habit_cost`): first, with H and R from before the day, each habit gets D = max(0, |A − H| − T) and P = min(P_max, alpha × alpha_factor × R^gamma × D^gamma) (T 0.10, alpha 200, gamma 2, P_max 100 per habit, `alpha_factor` 0 for sleep and 1 for activity, strength and screens). Changes up to T cost nothing, two small steps cost less than one big one (gamma 2), cutting a habit costs as much as adding one, sleep is free, and a well-rooted habit (high R) costs most: on Very hard (R 0.8) activity 0 → 1 in one day costs 200 × 0.8² × 0.9² ≈ 104, capped at 100, a step of 0.2 about 1.3; the same jump costs about 41 on Normal (R 0.5) and 6.5 on Hard (R 0.2). The day's total is the sum over the habits, with no cap. This is the solution of the gradual-vs-sudden review (user decision; Google Doc "Habit-change cost: gradual vs sudden change"): a sudden change makes mental fall on Normal and empties mood on Very hard, while a 10-day change never lets it fall. In normal play this is strong: a Normal child doing 1 h of cardio and 1 h of entertainment from day 1 has mental 0.36 on day 1 (0.62 before), and a Very hard avatar doing 1 h of exercise from day 1 keeps mood at 0 and reaches the failure line on day 3, so Very hard needs short sessions at first. Then mood += `mood_speed` (0.3) × (target − mood) − total, kept between 0 and 100, with target = 100 × the average of the sleep, activity, screens and enjoyment scores. Mood is not multiplied by k. The day's costs are kept in `GameState.habit_costs` and `habit_cost_total` and shown in the debug panel. A Milestone 3 save's mood (0 to 1) is scaled to 0 to 100.
  - Usual level H of each habit (sleep, activity, strength, screens) is an **intensity** A from 0 to 1 (`health.habit_intensity`: sleep hours ÷ 12, moderate-to-vigorous minutes ÷ 60, strength days in the last 7 ÷ 7, screen hours ÷ 6, each at most 1; `Habits.intensities()`). Per real day H += rate × (A − H), rate = 1 − (1 − 0.95)^(1 ÷ 66) ≈ 0.044; run k times, so H reaches 95% of a new habit after about 13 avatar days. It starts at the difficulty's `start_H`.
  - Rootedness R of each habit: per real day R += lambda × (1 − R) (`habit_cost.lambda` 0.04), but **only on real days when the habit is stable** (|A − H| at most T), so a change in progress does not become more expensive every day; run k times. A habit missing from the day's intensities counts as stable. It starts at the difficulty's `start_R` (Easy 0.5, Normal 0.5, Hard 0.2, Very hard 0.8). H and R are saved; a Milestone 3 save (no R, and H as scores) loads with the Normal `start_H` and `start_R`.
  - Weight z-score stays at the difficulty's value (no food yet).
  - The Milestone 2 per-hour effects on muscle, physical, mental and sleep are gone, and so is the sleep measure falling per hour awake. Exercise still adds calories spent per hour, as a record only.
- **How the measures and health work** (`GameState`, numbers in the `health` block): right after the body states, once per avatar day:
  - Physical = (fitness + muscle + weight score) ÷ 3 (`physical_mix`, `weight_score`).
  - Sleep = the sleep score of the sleep that just ended × (1 − sleep debt ÷ `debt_max`), never below 0; debt_max = 10 h. So 10 h of debt or more make it 0 (for a child, one night without sleep gives 9 h). The 0-hour rule gives a sleep score of 0, so the sleep measure becomes 0.
  - Mental = mood ÷ 100 (the habit-change cost is already taken off mood). The Milestone 3 rules (`habit_change_cost` per difficulty and the old mood weights) are deleted. Doing much more than usual costs mental health; changing step by step costs little; Very hard pays twice as much, Hard half.
  - Target health = the weighted power mean of the three measures; displayed health += `alpha_bar` (0.37) × (target − health). Each change of displayed health gives or takes XP; the XP over time uses the displayed health level.
  - Before the first sleep the measures are the difficulty's starting measures.
  - **Failure** (Milestone 4 Task 6; there is no death, `death_enabled` false): when the displayed health is at or below `health.failure_at_health` (1.2, placeholder, set by the user after the Task 7 simulation; 1.05 before; the bar only comes close to the minimum 1), `GameState.is_at_failure()` is true and the speech bubble shows a placeholder failure message ("Failure: my health has reached the minimum."). Nothing else happens; the failure effect is not decided. The message goes away when health rises again.
  - **Debug view and CSV log** (Milestone 4 Task 8, debug builds only, never in the published game): the debug panel (F7) shows the five habit scores, sleep debt, fitness, muscle, the weight z-score, H and R of every habit, mood, the habit cost of the day (per habit and in total), the three measures, the target and displayed health. `HealthLog` (`scripts/autoload/health_log.gd`) writes one CSV line per scoring day to `user://health_log.csv` (header on the first line): scoring day, clock day, difficulty, age group, the day's totals, the five scores, A, H and R of each habit, the habit cost, sleep debt, fitness, muscle, z, mood, the three measures, target and displayed health, level and XP. On the Mac the file is in `~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Plasticine Tamagotchi/health_log.csv`. It is off while a test script runs (tests use their own file).
  - `tests/measures_test.gd` plays avatar days with the main screen open and checks after each sleep that the three measures, the target and the displayed health follow these formulas and that the health bar, level box and measures panel show them.
- **Simulation tests** (`tests/calibration_test.gd`, Milestone 4 Task 7): simulates 60 avatar days with fixed habits on each difficulty (a child), through the real action log and daily update, prints health on days 0, 1, 3, 5, 10, 20, 30, 45 and 60, and PASS or FAIL per scenario and difficulty. The p in config runs first and decides the result; then the whole run is printed again with p = 0.2, 0.5 and 1 for comparison. The team's scenarios (research plan, Phase 1) and how the test reads them: athlete who sleeps 5 h (2 h exercise a day) never goes above 3 (or above its start, on Easy) and ends at 3 or less; sedentary who sleeps well (no exercise, 4 h screens) ends below the perfect player and never reaches the minimum; perfect player (sleep in range, 60 min a day, strength 3 days a week, 2 h screens) reaches level 5 (health 4.5) but not before day 10; gradual change (from the difficulty's `start_H` to perfect in 10 days) has a higher average mental over days 1 to 15 than the sudden change; player who ignores everything (never sleeps, no exercise, 6 h screens) reaches the minimum (`failure_at_health`) but not before day 3; Very hard with perfect habits recovers (reaches health 3). When a scenario fails, no value is changed by Claude: the numbers and proposals go to the team. **Results (Task 7):** with p = 0.5, athlete, sedentary and Very hard recovery passed on every difficulty; perfect failed on Easy (level 5 on day 5) and on Hard and Very hard (never: the weight score stays low until food exists, so physical cannot reach 1); gradual vs sudden failed everywhere; ignores everything never reached the minimum. **The user then tried p = 0.1** (ignores everything passed everywhere, but the athlete who sleeps 5 h fell to about 1.05) **and settled on p = 0.2, with `failure_at_health` 1.2.** **Gradual vs sudden** was then solved (Google Doc "Habit-change cost: gradual vs sudden change", user decision): its criterion now follows the concept ("a gradual transition keeps mental health stable; without one mental health falls"): a 10-day change never lets mental fall below its starting value; on Normal and Very hard (`COSTLY_CHANGE`) the sudden change falls in the first 10 days and the 10-day change keeps a higher lowest mental; and a 40-day change has a lower 30-day average than the 10-day one. A higher average for the gradual change is not asked for: better habits sooner do help sooner. With the new habit cost (gamma 2, alpha 200, no cap on the total, R growing only while stable, free sleep) it passes on every difficulty (Normal: sudden falls from 0.50 to 0.41, gradual lowest 0.53; Very hard: sudden reaches 0, gradual lowest 0.15). Results now: athlete, sedentary, gradual vs sudden and Very hard recovery pass on every difficulty; ignores everything passes on Easy, Normal and Hard but fails "not at once" on Very hard (starts at 1.25, just above the failure line 1.2); perfect fails on Easy (day 6), Hard and Very hard (never, until food exists; criterion kept by the user). 4 checks fail in total.
- Normal start check: measures 0.5 · 0.5 · 0.5 give health 3. The old start values (physical 0.5, mental 0.5, sleep 1) would give 3.6, which is why Normal now starts with sleep 0.5.

**Food as an activity (built in Milestone 10 Task 3).** The Eat button opens Healthy meal and Fast-food meal (0.5 avatar hours each, `data/activities.json`; food values in `data/foods.json`) and, when the avatar is hungry, Skip meal. `scripts/health/energy.gd` gives the Schofield BMR, the reference appetite (BMR at the WHO healthy weight x `energy.pal_ref`), the healthy portion (reference / 4) and the kcal spent per step (BMR / 24 x the METy of the running activity, `mety` in `activities.json`, or `energy.mety_idle`). `scripts/health/hunger.gd` grows hunger (kcal) every step with the v1 formula (with d from `scripts/health/sleep_model.gd`, from the Milestone 4 debt until Task 6, and z from the Milestone 4 `weight_z` until Task 4), says "I'm hungry!" at 0.8 healthy meals after 2 h since the last meal or skip, and eats a meal when it ends, in proportion to the time eaten (Stop = part of a meal). Meals are logged with `kcal_eaten`. A new avatar starts with weight from its z at the WHO median height for the reference age, and ready for breakfast. Simulation is listed before Activities in the autoloads and splits a frame at the end of a day activity, so each step uses the right METy. **Energy, weight and body size (Milestone 10 Task 4):** every step the kcal spent go into a 24-slot energy window (one slot per avatar hour) and take weight off; a meal's kcal go into the window and onto the weight: weight += `body.k` x kcal / `body.kcal_per_kg` (`scripts/health/energy.gd`). The energy deficit penalty U (`GameState.deficit_u`) = 0 below a 30% deficit in the window, 1 at 60%; a new avatar's window starts in balance. `scripts/health/body_size.gd` ages the body by `body.k` x hours (stopping at `body.max_age_months` of the age group), sets height to the WHO median and z (`GameState.weight_z`) from weight / height², and gives the v1 weight score w(z) (`health.weight_score_points`). The starting z is the v1 one (`health.difficulties.<id>.z`); the Milestone 4 physical measure still uses its own weight score of this z until Task 8. Entertainment has **Screens** (METy 1.3, screen time) and **Football** (METy 7.5, moderate-to-vigorous); `kcal_per_hour` is gone, and the calories-spent record is the energy spent during activities whose intensity is in `energy.record_intensities`. U is only stored for now (its effects come in Tasks 5, 7 and 8).

**Training (built in Milestone 10 Task 5, `scripts/health/training.gd`, v1 Step 6).** Fitness and strength (`muscle` is renamed `strength`) are trained **every step**, no longer once per day by the Milestone 4 code. Cardio and Football count as cardio, Strength as strength (`train.cardio_types`, `train.strength_types`). Daily capacity = (60 + 90 x fitness) x (1 - 0.3 x d) minutes per avatar day (reset after any sleep and by the 0-hour rule); minutes beyond it give no effective minutes and build the overreaching load O (`GameState.overreach`: excess minutes per day averaged over 72 h, / 120). Recovery is clock time: cardio tires fully after 120 continuous minutes and recovers with a 6 h time constant; strength tires after 45 minutes and recovers with 24 h (86% after 48 h); a strength session that starts below 60% recovery is too soon: it gives no effective minutes and all its minutes build O (user decision, 25 Oct 2026; v1 predicted strength 0.34 for strength twice a day, and without this rule the model gave 0.75, more than the guideline plan). Effective minutes = minutes x performance (minigame default score 1, lowered by tiredness from `scripts/health/sleep_model.gd`) x recovery; their sum over the last 24 avatar hours (`scripts/health/hour_window.gd`, a sliding window shared with the energy window) gives A_cardio and A_strength. Targets: 0.1 + 0.9 x (1 - e^(-2 x minutes / ref)), x (1 - 0.5 x U); fitness and strength move toward them with 30 and 42 body days (k applies); only gains are cut by d, U and O. A new avatar's window holds the minutes that give its starting fitness and strength as targets, so nothing moves before the player acts. Starting fitness and strength come from the v1 difficulty table. The Milestone 4 calibration test no longer trains fitness (it skips the step loop); it is rewritten in Task 10.

**Sleep (built in Milestone 10 Task 6, `scripts/health/sleep_model.gd`, v1 Step 9).** At each new avatar day (when any sleep ends, or by the 0-hour rule as 0 h) Simulation runs `SleepModel.new_day()`: debt = debt x e^(-(slept / target) x 24 / (5 days x 24 / k)) + max(0, minimum - slept), with the range and target of the age group (child 9 to 12 h, target 10.5; teenager 8 to 10 h, target 9); k applies to the recovery, so a full night repays 86% and no sleep repays nothing; 6 h every night settles at 4.4 h. Sleep measure = (1 - min(1, debt / 8)) x (1 - min(0.3, 0.1 x hours above the maximum)); night quality is stored for happiness (Task 7). The starting debt comes from the v1 difficulty table, so Normal starts with 4 h of debt (Sleep 0.5) and is sleepy at once. "I'm tired" (`GameState.tired`) now comes from tiredness = max(d, (hours awake - 15) / 4) >= 0.5, worked out every step; the Clock no longer sets it. The Milestone 4 sleep debt rule and sleep measure are removed. While SaveGame loads a save, `Simulation.loading` is true, so the Clock's repeated phase change does not run a new day again.

**Diet and Happiness (built in Milestone 10 Task 7, `scripts/health/diet.gd` and `scripts/health/happiness.gd`, v1 Steps 7 and 10).** `GameState.mental` is renamed `happiness` (UI: "Happiness"). Each meal moves diet quality Q toward x (1 healthy, 0 fast food), craving C and habituation A toward 1 - x, by 1 / (time constant x 4 meals): Q 21 and C 30 real days (body time, divided by k), A 5 avatar days (clock time); a part of a meal moves them by that share. Happiness moves every step toward its baseline (0.15 + 0.30 Physical + 0.25 Sleep + 0.10 Q + 0.20 Fun - 0.2 U - 0.10 screen excess - 0.1 O) with a 12-hour time constant (clock time) and jumps with the v1 events: meals (healthy +0.01 - 0.06 C, fast food +0.01 + 0.06 C (1 - A), using C and A from before the meal), starting cardio or strength (-0.03 (1 - fitness)), exercise within capacity (+0.07 per hour, less when tired) or beyond it (-0.02 per hour), football (+0.10 per hour), screens (+0.05 per hour, only the first 120 minutes of a day), 4 awake hours without screens (-0.02), hungry while awake above 1.2 meals (-0.05 per hour), waking (+0.08 (night quality - 0.5)) and being kept awake at tiredness 1 (-0.02 per hour). Fun and screen excess use a sliding 24-hour window (`mood.fun` types: football, screens, exercise). The starting happiness, Q, C and A come from the v1 difficulty table. **Deleted in Task 7:** the Milestone 4 mood (0 to 100), the habit-change cost (T, alpha, gamma, P_max, H, R), the daily habit scores, `body_test.gd` and the old `calibration_test.gd` (Task 10 writes the new one). `GameState.update_body_states()` became `daily_update()` (Milestone 4 Physical and the health bar step, until Task 8) and `Habits.scores_updated` became `day_closed`. `health_m4` keeps only what that code still reads. `tests/happiness_test.gd` plays v1 scenarios for 21 days, eating whenever the avatar is hungry: the guideline plan ends at happiness 0.92; with craving 0.9 an abrupt switch to healthy food drops to 0.41 at the lowest (v1 0.40) and a gradual one to 0.44 (v1 0.50).

**Physical, health, failure, risk, XP and the avatar's looks (built in Milestone 10 Task 8).** `scripts/health/health_model.gd`: Physical (v1 Step 8) every step; target health = 1 + 4 x the power mean (p 0.5, equal weights) of Physical, Happiness and Sleep; the displayed health moves 37% of the way to it when a sleep ends; failure timers (health below 1.3 for 24 h, or happiness below 0.10 for 12 h) and the placeholder message "Failure: my health or happiness has stayed too low for too long." `scripts/health/risk.gd`: risk index R and hidden damage, shown only in the debug panel. `scripts/health/progress.gd`: v1 XP. `scripts/health/avatar_states.gd`: hungry, tired, sad (happiness below 0.3), thin (z below -2), overweight (z above +1), muscular (strength 0.7 or more; with overweight = muscular with fat); the egg is narrower when thin and wider when overweight, has a thicker outline when muscular, heavy eyelids when tired and a frown when sad, and the speech bubble says "I'm sad." The physical values box shows calories spent, strength, fitness, weight, height, BMI-for-age z and diet quality. The starting health matches the v1 table for a boy of 10: Easy 3.94, Normal 2.91, Hard 1.95, Very hard 1.49 (1.35 in v1; happiness 0.30, decision 7). **The Milestone 4 health block (`health_m4`), the old XP rules and the `thresholds` group are deleted;** the action-log settings moved to the config group `log`.
